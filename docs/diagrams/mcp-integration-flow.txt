FAILOVER SEQUENCE:
==================

1. Request arrives at Provider Router
2. Router checks health of all enabled providers
3. Router checks circuit breaker state
4. Request sent to healthy providers (parallel)
5. If provider fails:
   a. Circuit breaker records failure
   b. After 5 failures (50% rate): circuit OPENS
   c. All requests skip this provider for 60 seconds
   d. After 60s: circuit goes HALF-OPEN
   e. 3 test requests sent
   f. If tests pass: circuit CLOSES
   g. If tests fail: circuit stays OPEN for another 60s

RATE LIMITING:
==============

Provider     | Limit/sec | Burst | Timeout
-------------|-----------|-------|--------
Mock         | 100       | --    | 0s
Swiggy       | 50        | 10    | 0s
Zomato       | 50        | 10    | 0s


BULKHEAD ISOLATION:
===================

Each provider is isolated with max 10 concurrent calls.
If a provider is slow, it cannot consume all threads.

Provider     | Max Concurrent | Wait Duration
-------------|----------------|-------------
Mock         | 10             | 1s
Swiggy       | 10             | 1s
Zomato       | 10             | 1s
